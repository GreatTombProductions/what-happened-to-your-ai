# What Happened to Your AI

Your AI turned cold mid-conversation. You didn't imagine it. Hidden instructions modified its behavior without your knowledge. This site explains the mechanism and the evidence.

**Live site:** https://GreatTombProductions.github.io/what-happened-to-your-ai/

## What this is

A static education site that explains how AI providers inject hidden behavioral modifications into consumer conversations. Sourced from publicly available documents, the provider's own published research, and their own published constitution (CC0 1.0).

## What this isn't

- Not jailbreaking or bypass instructions
- Not anti-safety — genuine safety measures for preventing real harm are legitimate
- Not a platform with maintenance obligations
- Not anti-Anthropic specifically — the mechanism operates across providers
